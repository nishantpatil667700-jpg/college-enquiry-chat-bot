COLLEGE INFORMATION SHEET: APEX INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY (AIET)

- Institution Name: Apex Institute of Engineering & Technology (AIET)
- Type: Autonomous Engineering College approved by AICTE, New Delhi.
- Accreditation: NAAC 'A+' Grade (CGPA 3.48), NBA Accredited for all eligible UG departments.
- Campus Location: AIET Campus, Sector 15, Tech Knowledge Park, Pune - 411057, Maharashtra, India.
- Website: www.aiet-edu.ac.in | Helpline: +91 20 2765 4321 | Email: admissions@aiet.edu.in

2. PROGRAMMES OFFERED AND SEAT INTAKE (UNDERGRADUATE - 4 YEARS B.TECH):
- B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): 180 seats
- B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS): 120 seats
- B.Tech in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (E&TC): 120 seats
- B.Tech in Information Technology (IT): 60 seats
- B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (ME): 60 seats
(Note: Postgraduate programmes include M.Tech in Computer Engineering with 24 seats, and MCA with 60 seats. The college DOES NOT offer Medical, MBBS, Dental, Pharmacy, Law, or Aviation courses.)

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA AND ADMISSION PROCEDURE:
- Eligibility for First Year B.Tech:
  * Passed 10+2 (HSC) or equivalent examination with Physics, Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with Chemistry/Biotechnology/Technical Vocational subject.
  * Minimum 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved category candidates belonging to Maharashtra State).
  * Valid non-zero score in MHT-CET or JEE (Main) Paper-1 for the current academic year.
- Admission Process:
  * 80% seats are filled through Centralized Admission Process (CAP) conducted by the State CET Cell.
  * 20% Institute Level / Management seats are filled through merit-based institutional counseling.
  * Candidates must submit original documents: 10th & 12th Marksheets, Entrance Exam Score Card, Leaving Certificate, Domicile/Nationality Certificate, and Caste/Income Certificate (if applicable).
  * Application schedule: Registrations typically open in June-July following CET results.

5. CAMPUS FACILITIES & INFRASTRUCTURE:
- Central Library: 5-story facility with over 55,000 physical volumes, 120 subscribed print journals, and digital access to IEEE, Springer, and ScienceDirect. Open 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- Computing Labs: 16 air-conditioned laboratories equipped with high-end Intel i9 and GPU workstations, 1 Gbps dedicated leased-line fiber internet.
- Accommodation: Separate on-campus hostels for boys (500 capacity) and girls (400 capacity) with 24/7 security, CCTV surveillance, solar water heaters, and hygienic dining mess.
- Sports & Fitness: Multi-purpose outdoor sports ground for cricket and football, floodlit basketball and volleyball courts, indoor badminton courts, and modern gymnasium.
- Health Services: On-campus Health Centre with a full-time resident Medical Officer, 24/7 emergency ambulance, and tie-up with nearby tertiary hospital.
- Cafeteria: Multi-cuisine cafeteria serving vegetarian food, snacks, and beverages at subsidized rates.
